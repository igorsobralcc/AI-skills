# Platform Engineering

## Golden rules

- Identify internal customers, repeated friction, desired outcomes, service levels, ownership, and support model.
- Provide self-service APIs, templates, documentation, observability, secure defaults, and measured adoption.
- Keep paved roads opinionated around proven common needs and provide intentional escape hatches.
- Fund platform reliability, lifecycle, migration, and feedback like a product.

## Guardrails

- Do not centralize every decision, mandate immature abstractions, or create a platform before demand is demonstrated.
- Avoid hiding cost, failures, or ownership behind self-service.
- Platform work does not authorize organization-wide policy changes without approval.
