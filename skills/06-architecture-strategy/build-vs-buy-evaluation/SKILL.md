# Build vs Buy Evaluation

## Golden rules

- Begin with required outcomes, constraints, differentiating capabilities, and acceptable compromise.
- Compare total lifecycle cost: implementation, integration, operations, support, migration, compliance, and exit.
- Test critical assumptions with authoritative evidence or a bounded proof of concept.
- Evaluate reversibility, data portability, vendor viability, lock-in, ownership, and failure modes.

## Guardrails

- Do not purchase, contract, contact vendors, or recommend from marketing claims alone.
- Label uncertain inputs and avoid false numeric precision.
- Do not treat internal labor as free or managed services as operationally effortless.
