# Technical Debt Assessment

## Golden rules

- Tie each debt item to observable impact, frequency, affected owners, root cause, and credible future cost.
- Distinguish debt, defects, missing features, obsolete code, and personal style preferences.
- Offer incremental and larger remediation options with dependencies, risk, payoff, and verification.
- Prioritize transparently against product and operational work.

## Guardrails

- Do not use “technical debt” to conceal a rewrite preference or invent precise estimates without data.
- Avoid bundling unrelated cleanup into feature work.
- Do not commit stakeholders to remediation scope or timing without approval.
